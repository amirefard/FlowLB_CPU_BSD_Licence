Case 10 is a Poiseuille flow with heat transfer in a 2D domain.  According to Zuo and He (1997) we have G=2\rho \nu u_max / L^2 where L is the half channel height. 
In case of constant velocity inlet u_in, the u_max after forming the Poiseuille flow must be u_max = 1.5 u_in.

Also the Nusselt number must approach 7.54 (not very close to the channel end where T=1 everywhere). Nu = D_h/(Tw - Tb) * dT/dY. And D_h = 2 * H
Tb = \int{Ux.T}_0^H / \int{Ux}_0^H

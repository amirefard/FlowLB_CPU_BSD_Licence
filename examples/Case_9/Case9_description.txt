Case 9 is a Poiseuille flow in a 2D domain. BC: X_velIn_ZeroGradOut. According to Zuo and He (1997) we have G=2\rho \nu u_max / L^2 where L is the half channel height. 
In case of constant velocity inlet u_in, the u_max after forming the Poiseuille flow must be u_max = 1.5 u_in.

The code worked very well with both X_velIn_PressureOut (Using Zou-He for the outlet and rho_out=1.0) and also with X_velIn_ZeroGradOut (with NEQ extrapolation scheme in the outlet)
